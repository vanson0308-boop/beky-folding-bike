Youth, Skyline or NANO?

Start with your daily rides and where you will store the bike, then compare wheel sizes and gearing.

Youth

For everyday short rides, start by exploring the 16-inch Youth. Its 6-speed setup offers a starting point for getting to know Beky folding bikes.

View specifications and colours

Skyline

If you prefer a wide-tyre setup, explore Skyline. Its 18 × 2.125 tyres and 7-speed drivetrain offer a different configuration within this trio.

View specifications and colours

NANO

If folded storage space matters most, take a look at the 14-inch NANO. Check whether its smaller wheels and 4-speed setup suit your needs.

View specifications and colours

Before ordering, confirm the weight, folded dimensions, colour and lead time of your chosen version. Measure your storage space if you plan to carry it upstairs or place it in a car.

About

A folding bike is not about how fast you arrive. It is about making “going out” feel as natural as breathing. Take it onto the metro, tuck it into the trunk, or lean it outside your favourite café window. Beky believes every street holds countless stories. We simply want to give you one more reason to open the door and step outside.
